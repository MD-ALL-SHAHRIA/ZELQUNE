# Zelqune product brief (for the promo video)

**What it is.** zelqune.com, an online store for handcrafted wood and soft-light wall decor, made to
order in Dhaka and delivered across Bangladesh. Sculptural panels, shelves and lamps that turn a
plain wall into the warm centre of a room.

**Who it's for.** Bangladeshi home owners and renters (living room, bedroom), plus cafés and
offices (interior design, construction and bulk/corporate orders).

**Core pain.** Bare, cold walls under harsh white tube light; mass-produced decor that looks like
everyone else's; distrust of paying online in full for something you have not seen.

**Most persuasive features (all real, from the storefront).**
1. A curated shop of sculptural pieces (Alo-Arch Panel ৳3,490, Capsule Vase Light ৳4,490, Fluted
   Niche Panel ৳8,990, Half-Moon Planter Light ৳6,990).
2. One piece, two moods: "A vase by day. A soft lamp by night." Safe 5V USB light, no 220V in the wood.
3. Low-risk pre-order: reserve with ৳300 by bKash or Nagad, made and checked within 10 days, pay
   the rest in cash on delivery.
4. Order tracking by order code and mobile number; delivery to all 64 districts.

**Real numbers.** ৳300 advance · 10 days to make · 64 districts · 5V safe light.
