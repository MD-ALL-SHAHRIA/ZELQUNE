# Privacy, security, accessibility and legal audit (2026-10-31)

Scope: storefront (`frontend`), admin (`dashboard`), gateway and services (`backend`), as they are
in `main` at `1e4e9dd`. Every finding below comes from reading the code or running the site;
nothing here is legal advice, and no part of the site is claimed to be "compliant".

## 1. How the business and the site actually work

- **Business:** Zelqune, handcrafted wood and light wall decor, made in Dhaka, sold to customers
  in Bangladesh (B2C pre-orders and in-stock items; B2B bulk quotes; interior design and
  construction leads). English and Bangla. No customer accounts yet (the account page points to
  code + phone lookups).
- **Payments:** no card gateway. Customers pay a ৳300 advance by **bKash or Nagad** (Send Money)
  and type the **Transaction ID**; staff verify it by hand; the rest is **cash on delivery**. No
  card or wallet credentials ever touch the site.
- **Infrastructure:** self-hosted Postgres (one schema per service), Redis, RabbitMQ; Next.js
  storefront and dashboard; product/content media on local disk in dev, Cloudflare R2 planned.
  SMS driver is `console` (no SMS provider; nothing is sent).

## 2. Findings

### Critical

- **C1. Customers' phone numbers go in URLs and therefore into server logs.** Order and quote
  tracking call `GET /v1/orders/track?code=…&phone=…` and `GET /v1/quote-requests/track?…`. The
  gateway logs every request URL (`pino-http` auto logging; `redact` covers only auth/cookie
  headers), so phone numbers are written to logs and also end up in proxy/CDN logs and browser
  history. **Fix:** send the phone in a POST body (or header), keep GET for the code only, and
  redact query strings in request logs.
- **C2. Meta Pixel loads without consent.** `site-analytics.tsx` injects the Pixel on every page as
  soon as `NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID` is set; purchase/lead events fire through it. It is dormant
  today, so the risk appears the day it is switched on. **Fix:** load it only after marketing
  consent; never send name/phone/address to it.
- **C3. The current Privacy text is inaccurate.** It says cookies are used "only to keep your cart
  and language" and that visits are measured "without storing personal details"; in fact the site
  sets a 365-day analytics id, campaign cookies and a price-experiment cookie (language is in the
  URL, not a cookie). It also states a 5-year retention that nobody has decided (this was draft
  wording). A policy that misdescribes processing is itself a liability. **Fix:** rewrite from the
  inventory above; mark retention periods as owner/lawyer decisions until confirmed.

### High

- **H1. No consent mechanism; analytics cookies are set before any choice.** `proxy.ts` sets
  `zq_sid`/`zq_utm` on the first request. Whether Bangladesh law requires prior consent for
  first-party analytics must be confirmed by a lawyer; visitors from the EU/UK clearly require it.
  **Fix:** consent banner (Accept all / Reject all / Customize), set analytics/marketing cookies and
  send analytics events only after consent; footer "Cookie settings" to change it later.
- **H2. Price experiment shows different prices to different visitors.** `zq_p` assigns arm a/b and
  the catalog serves arm-specific prices (`?p=a|b`). Consumer-protection and fairness questions
  (disclosure, honouring the lower price, discrimination) need a lawyer and an owner decision
  before production. **Proposed default:** disclose it in the Terms/Cookie policy and, unless the
  lawyer clears it, run it only with consent or switch it off at launch.
- **H3. No privacy-request or retention process.** There is no way for a customer to ask for
  access, correction or deletion, and nothing ever deletes or anonymises data (orders, leads,
  customer flags and analytics events are kept indefinitely; abandoned carts are cleaned).
  **Fix:** a privacy request form (verified by order/quote code + phone, then a staff call), a
  dashboard queue with deadlines, documented retention per data type, and an anonymisation job for
  data past retention (keeping what tax/accounting law requires).
- **H4. Forms have no privacy notice.** Checkout and the three lead forms collect name, phone and
  more without telling the person how it is used. **Fix:** one short notice next to each submit
  button linking to the Privacy Policy. There is no marketing messaging today, so **no marketing
  checkbox is added**; if WhatsApp/SMS marketing starts, add a separate, unticked opt-in then.
- **H5. Business identity is missing.** No legal entity name, registered/trade address, trade
  licence number, VAT registration (BIN) or support email/phone is confirmed in the code or
  settings. Bangladesh's digital-commerce rules are generally understood to expect seller
  identity, contact and policies to be displayed (a lawyer must confirm what applies). **Fix:**
  add these fields to Dashboard → Settings → Store details and show them in the footer and legal
  pages only when filled (never invented).

### Medium

- **M1. Terms, Returns and a Cookie policy are thin or missing.** Terms is 6 bullets; Returns has
  no full page content of its own; there is no Cookie policy. The change-of-mind return rule
  ("3 days, unused, original box") was draft wording and is **not an owner decision yet**.
- **M2. Unsplash hot-linking** sends every visitor's IP to Unsplash. Licence is fine (Unsplash
  License) but it is a third-party transfer to disclose; self-host or replace with own photos.
- **M3. Sample photos stand in for products.** They carry "Sample photo" notes in most places;
  keep that disclosure everywhere a stock photo could be mistaken for the product.
- **M4. CSP allows `'unsafe-inline'` scripts** (needed by inline bootstrap scripts). Moving to
  nonces would harden it. Other headers are good: CSP, HSTS (production), X-Frame-Options DENY,
  nosniff, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy.
- **M5. Request logs** keep IPs and URLs with no stated retention. Set a log retention period.

### Low

- **L1. Accessibility** is already strong on audited pages (Lighthouse accessibility 100 on home,
  product, shop, design; skip link; `lang` per locale; reduced-motion support; contrast fixed on
  2026-10-28). Not yet tested with automated axe checks across every page, nor keyboard-only
  through the new hero slides, the construction journey and the dashboard.
- **L2. Footer copyright** reads "© 2026 Zelqune. All rights reserved." — fine once the legal name
  is confirmed (it should use the registered name if different).

### Things that are already right (keep)

Admin auth (ES256 short-lived tokens, rotating HttpOnly SameSite=Strict refresh, per-IP and
per-phone login limits); rate limits on checkout, tracking and RFQ; Turnstile on public forms;
Idempotency keys on orders; Zod validation on every input; no card data handled; pino redacts auth
headers and cookies; analytics events carry no name or phone; owner-phone logs are masked.

## 5. Questions for a qualified lawyer (Bangladesh)

1. Which data-protection instrument is currently in force (the Personal Data Protection Ordinance
   2025 / any 2026 Act), its effective dates, and the exact obligations for a small e-commerce
   business (lawful bases, consent, notices, rights, response deadlines, breach notification,
   registration, cross-border transfer to Cloudflare/Meta).
2. Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines (2021, as amended) and the National Digital Commerce
   Policy: required seller disclosures, delivery/refund timelines, advance-payment rules for
   pre-orders, cancellation rights.
3. Consumer Rights Protection Act 2009: pricing display, the price experiment, "made to order"
   cancellation and refund terms, warranty statements (e.g. construction "1-year workmanship").
4. Cookie consent: whether prior consent is required for first-party analytics in Bangladesh;
   EU/UK visitors (GDPR/UK GDPR) if marketing targets them.
5. Retention: minimum periods under the VAT and Income Tax laws for invoices and order records.
6. Construction and interior-design services: licensing and liability language for the Terms.

## 6. Proposed implementation plan (for approval)

1. **Critical fixes:** tracking lookups via POST with the phone in the body (+ query redaction in
   logs); Pixel only with marketing consent.
2. **Consent system:** accessible banner (Accept all / Reject all / Customize), categories
   Necessary / Analytics / Marketing, versioned `zq_consent` cookie (6 months), proxy and analytics
   only set `zq_sid`/`zq_utm` and send events with analytics consent, Pixel with marketing consent,
   "Cookie settings" in the footer; one cookie registry that also renders the Cookie Policy table.
   Price experiment handled per your answer (default: off until cleared).
3. **Legal pages (EN + BN):** Privacy Policy (rewritten from §1), Terms & Conditions, Refund,
   Return & Cancellation Policy, Cookie Policy. Business details come from Settings and are hidden
   when empty; undecided rules are shown as clearly marked "to be confirmed" in the dashboard
   preview only, never as invented facts. Each page lists "last updated".
4. **Forms:** privacy notice beside every submit button; no pre-ticked boxes; no new data fields.
5. **Privacy requests:** public request form (type, code + phone, details) → backend request queue
   with deadlines → dashboard page to verify, act (export, correct, anonymise) and record the
   outcome; retention rules documented, anonymisation keeps tax/accounting records.
6. **Business details:** new Settings fields (legal name, address, trade licence, BIN, support
   email/phone) shown in footer and legal pages.
7. **Accessibility:** automated axe checks across all pages (EN/BN, desktop/phone) in the e2e
   suite, keyboard-only pass through header, hero, journey, checkout, banner and dashboard; fix
   what they find.
8. **Asset inventory:** this table kept in `docs/compliance/assets.md`; replace anything the owner
   cannot confirm.
9. **Tests:** consent persistence and withdrawal; no analytics cookie/event/Pixel before consent;
   form notices; privacy request flow; axe; broken-link crawl; full regression suites.
