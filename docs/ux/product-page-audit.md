# Product page: UX audit and redesign (2026-10-26)

Method, after the owner's reference (Apex product page case study by Md. Minhazul Abedin):
UX audit, competitor review, prioritise issues, define UX goals, then improve product
exploration and purchase confidence. Goal: make the piece easy to understand, the buying
decision clear, and doubt about delivery and payment disappear at the moment of decision.

| # | Issue on our page | Change | Expected impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One tall arch image and two small crops: details (wood grain, the light, the glass) hard to inspect | Two-column grid of large photos on desktop (odd count: the first spans both); swipe strip on phones; every photo opens full screen | Better product understanding |
| 2 | Trust icons, a timeline, a district picker, a pre-order notice and accordions all stacked under the buttons, competing with the buying decision | Buy now / Pre-order now and Add to cart as the only primary actions; then one compact **Delivery across Bangladesh** card (lead time, pre-order terms, district fee) and one **Buy with confidence** card (pay the rest on delivery, refund if late, damage covered) | Less confusion, fewer abandoned carts |
| 3 | The size was buried in Details; "See it on your wall" was a small link under the photos | **Size** (from the finish's real dimensions) beside the finish choice, with **See it on your wall** right there | Clearer size decision, fewer fit-related returns |
| 4 | Nothing invited a second piece | **Complete the wall**: up to three related pieces sold the same way (a cart holds one sale mode), each with its live price and a one-tap Add | More multi-piece orders, higher order value |
| 5 | Details, delivery terms and care at the bottom of the buying column | Accordions under the gallery (Details, Made for you, Delivery and payment, Care) | Reduced clutter, focus on the purchase |
| 6 | No way to share a piece | One **Share** action beside the title (phone share sheet, else copy link) | Word of mouth |

Deliberately not copied: "N people viewed / N bought" counters. Zelqune never shows invented
or unverifiable social proof (frontend/CLAUDE.md). There is no wishlist feature, so none is shown.
